## About Me

I am a **Systems Analysis and Development** student focused on **Data Analytics**, **Business Intelligence**, and **Data Engineering**.

I build projects that cover the complete data lifecycle: ingestion, transformation, data-quality testing, dimensional modeling, and visualization. I work with **Python**, **Java** **SQL**, **Power BI**, and **DAX**.

My goal is to turn raw data into reliable, understandable, and actionable information for business decision-making.
